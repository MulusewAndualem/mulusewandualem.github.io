Biography & Background

About Me

Dr. Mulusew Andualem Asemahagn is an Associate Professor of Health Informatics and Public Health with over 15 years of academic, executive research, and strategic leadership experience. Currently serving as the Corporate Director of Research and Community Engagement at Bahir Dar University, he spearheads the university’s institutional research governance, community outreach, and research ethics framework.

His career bridges advanced health informatics implementations—such as Routine Health Information Systems (RHIS), electronic medical records (EMR), and telemedicine—with robust epidemiological frameworks, health system strengthening, and ethics committees (including the CMHS Ethics Review Board).
